Gusan on iPhone — plan and mockups, v1

Speak, and it's done. One bar above your keyboard, two modes (Smart, Agent), and an app that only has what the bar needs.

1. The one iOS rule that shapes everything

iOS does not let any app draw on top of another app's keyboard or screen. The only things that can live inside other apps are: a custom keyboard, the Share sheet, and Shortcuts triggers (Back Tap, Action button). So "Gusan on top of my own keyboard" is impossible as a layer.

Proposal: the Gusan bar. Gusan becomes a short keyboard (about half the height of the normal one) with only the Gusan controls, no letter keys. You keep typing on your own keyboard. One tap on the globe key switches to the Gusan bar; the globe on the bar switches back. Result: it feels like a bar that appears when you want it, and it's the only way to put text directly at your cursor.

Second way in, no keyboard needed: double-tap the back of the phone (or the Action button). Gusan takes a screenshot, listens, does the task, and the answer is copied to the clipboard with a notification. If the Gusan bar is open later, it inserts the answer for you.

2. The Gusan bar: every state

Shown inside a chat app. The bar is the only Gusan UI you see while working. Blue accent, liquid glass, no red.

4. How the two modes work

Smart (default)

Tap mic→chime, listen→Stop (chime)→Gusan Cloud: transcribe + understand→context: selected text, the field, screen frame, web if needed→insert at cursor · Undo

Examples: "reply that 3 works and ask for the numbers", "make this more formal", "translate this to Armenian", "what does this error mean?" (with screen), "write a short bio from my LinkedIn" (web). No preset buttons: your words are the action.

Agent (H Company computer sessions)

Switch chip to Agent→say the task (+ screen)→a computer session starts→progress in the bar and in Sessions→Confirm before anything irreversible→login requests via Vault→result: insert, or kept in Sessions

The session stays alive so you can say "now also book the return" from the bar or the chat. You can stop it any time. Memory per user: your preferences and past sessions, stored in Gusan Cloud, never on H.

6. Things only you can decide

  1. Bar instead of full keyboard. You switch to Gusan with the globe key and back the same way. OK, or do you still want letter keys as an option?
  2. Screen context default. Off until you turn it on (it shows iOS's red recording indicator while on), or on by default?
  3. Agent logins. Vault with Face ID and approve-every-time, as mocked? Payment cards stay out of v1.
  4. Back Tap. Include in phase 2, or later?

Known limits that stay: the first mic tap per session opens Gusan for a moment (iOS rule); the agent's cloud browser starts with no logins; a free Apple ID means builds expire after 7 days and notifications arrive only when the app checks, not instantly.
