# How to test Gusan live

Gusan is an iPhone keyboard (iOS 17+). We don't have an Apple Developer account yet, so it isn't on TestFlight. There are two ways to try it:

**1. Test it on our phone (easiest).** Find us at the event and we'll hand you an iPhone with Gusan already set up. Try Smart replies and the Agent live in WhatsApp or Notes.

**2. Install it on your own iPhone (about 10 minutes, needs a Mac or Windows PC and a cable):**
1. Download the app: **[link to Gusan-iOS.ipa]**
2. Install it with **Sideloadly** (sideloadly.io) or **AltStore** (altstore.io): connect your iPhone, drag in the .ipa and sign in with your Apple ID. A free Apple ID works, but apps installed this way only work for 7 days.
3. On the iPhone: Settings › General › VPN & Device Management › trust your Apple ID. On iOS 16+, also turn on Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode.
4. Open Gusan and sign in with the demo account (ask us for the login).
5. Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard › **Gusan** › turn on **Allow Full Access**.
6. In Notes or WhatsApp, hold the globe key and pick Gusan.

## What to try
- **Smart:** select a message or put the cursor in a reply, tap the mic, and say "reply that I'm free at 3". The text appears at the cursor, with Undo.
- **Agent:** tap the chip to switch to Agent, tap the mic, and say "find the best deal on an iPhone 15 Pro on Leboncoin". Follow it in the Dynamic Island. The result, with links to the listings, shows up in Sessions.
