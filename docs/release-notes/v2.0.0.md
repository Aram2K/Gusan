## Transcribe is now Gusan

Gusan 2.0.0 is the same app with a new name and a new look. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### 🪟 One window

- **One main window with pages:** Home, Dictation, Smart Actions, Meetings, Live Assistant, Audio to text, Dictionary and your account.
- **Settings on each page.** Each page has its settings right next to what they change, and changes apply at once.

### 🎙 A new setup

- **A few short steps.** The setup checks your computer, recommends a speech model, and helps you pick your microphone and shortcut.
- **Real model names.** Speech and AI models are listed by their real names, so you know what runs.

### ✍️ Smart Actions

- **Their own shortcut: Alt+S** (Option+S on a Mac). Alt+R always pastes your exact words.
- **Their own speech model,** if you want one. Pick it on the Smart Actions page.
- **Lists and notes paste with real formatting** in Word, Outlook, Gmail, Docs, Notion, Slack and Teams.

### 🎧 Meetings

- **Play a meeting right in the app.** Seek on the waveform, no other player needed.
- **Transcripts read in paragraphs,** old meetings too.

### 📄 Audio to text

- **Drop an audio or video file** and get a transcript, with speakers, timestamps and a Word file if you want them. Gusan tells you when it's ready.

### 🕶 Live Assistant

- **Live Assistant has its own page**, with its shortcut, past sessions and who writes the answers.
- **The card stays hidden when you share your screen.** On a Mac, macOS can't always promise this, and the card says so.

### 📅 Calendar

- **Google Calendar reminders.** When your calendar is connected, Gusan reminds you before a meeting and can record it in one click.

### ✨ Calmer Pro

- **No pop-ups while you work.** Pro features have a small label, and the Pro window opens only when you pick one.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
