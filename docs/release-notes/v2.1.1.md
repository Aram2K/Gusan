## Ask about a meeting, names from your screen, and fuller notes

Gusan 2.1.1 opens your meeting the moment Live Assistant stops, lets you ask it questions, writes fuller notes, reads who is on the call from your screen, and names the speakers much faster. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### 💬 Ask about this meeting

- **Stop opens the meeting.** When you stop Live Assistant, Gusan opens that meeting: the notes and the transcript, with a chat beside them.
- **Ask anything about it.** "What did we decide?", "What do I have to do?", "Write a follow-up email." Answers come from what was said in that meeting.
- **Your choice of AI.** Gusan Cloud answers are part of Gusan Pro. Your own key, or an AI on this computer, works for everyone. In Privacy Mode nothing leaves this computer.
- **Hidden on a screen share**, like the rest of Live Assistant. Each meeting keeps its own chat, and Clear chat removes it.

### 📝 Fuller notes

- **Notes grow with the meeting.** A short call gets a short summary. A long one gets an overview, the decisions, the action items and a Discussion section, topic by topic.
- **Clearer owners.** An action item says who owns it, never "Them" or "Speaker 2", and an empty "Owner:" line no longer appears.
- **Notes appear as they are written** with Gusan Cloud and with your own key.

### 👥 Names from your screen

- **With Screen on in Live Assistant**, Gusan also reads the names of the people on your call from the screen, a little after the meeting starts and again if the screen changes.
- **They appear under People**, and the notes, the answers and the chat spell them right. If the call is you and one other person, Gusan names that speaker in the transcript.
- **A wrong name?** Remove it with the × on its row. It stays removed.
- **Nothing is read in Privacy Mode**, and only an AI that can see images is used.

### ⚡ Faster speaker names

- **About 15 times faster** on a long call. Identifying the speakers of a 55-minute call took 2.5 minutes here instead of 37.
- **The banner tells the truth.** It shows real progress, says when it is only waiting, and goes away when the work ends.

### 🎬 Installing and starting

- **A short video** plays while Windows installs Gusan.
- **A small loading card** shows while Gusan starts on Windows.

### 📦 Downloads named Gusan

- **Windows installer.** `Gusan-Windows-Setup.exe`. The same file is also `TranscribeApp-Windows-Setup.exe`.
- **Windows portable.** `Gusan-Windows.zip`, also published as `TranscribeApp-Windows.zip`.
- **Mac.** `Gusan-Mac.dmg`, also published as `TranscribeApp-Mac.dmg`.
- **What gets installed stays as it was.** The program file is still `TranscribeApp.exe`, in the same folder, so this replaces the Gusan you already have. An older Gusan still finds the TranscribeApp download and updates.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
