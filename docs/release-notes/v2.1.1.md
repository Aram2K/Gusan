## Ask about a meeting, names from your screen, simpler Speech & AI

Gusan 2.1.1 opens your meeting the moment Live Assistant stops, lets you ask it questions, writes fuller notes, reads who is on the call from your screen, and names the speakers much faster. Speech & AI is simpler, any API key works, the Pro window is new, and Gusan looks and behaves like a proper Mac app. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### 💬 Ask about this meeting

- **Stop opens the meeting.** When you stop Live Assistant, Gusan opens that meeting: the notes and the transcript, with a chat beside them.
- **Ask anything about it.** "What did we decide?", "What do I have to do?", "Write a follow-up email." Answers come from what was said in that meeting.
- **Your choice of AI.** Gusan Cloud answers are part of Gusan Pro. Your own key, or an AI on this computer, works for everyone. In Privacy Mode nothing leaves this computer.
- **Hidden on a screen share**, like the rest of Live Assistant. Each meeting keeps its own chat, and Clear chat removes it.

### 📝 Fuller notes

- **Notes grow with the meeting.** A short call gets a short summary. A long one gets an overview, the decisions, the action items and a Discussion section, topic by topic.
- **Clearer owners.** An action item says who owns it, never "Them" or "Speaker 2", and an empty "Owner:" line no longer appears.
- **Notes appear as they are written** with Gusan Cloud and with your own key.

### 👥 Names from your screen

- **With Screen on in Live Assistant**, Gusan also reads the names of the people on your call from the screen, a little after the meeting starts and again if the screen changes.
- **They appear under People**, and the notes, the answers and the chat spell them right. If the call is you and one other person, Gusan names that speaker in the transcript.
- **A wrong name?** Remove it with the × on its row. It stays removed.
- **Nothing is read in Privacy Mode**, and only an AI that can see images is used.

### ⚡ Faster speaker names

- **About 15 times faster** on a long call. Identifying the speakers of a 55-minute call took 2.5 minutes here instead of 37.
- **The banner tells the truth.** It shows real progress, says when it is only waiting, and goes away when the work ends.

### 🎛️ Simpler Speech & AI

- **Two cards, one format.** Speech-to-text and Language model. Pick where it runs (this computer, Gusan Cloud or your own key), then one model. Nothing changes or downloads until you pick a model.
- **A Model dropdown on the Dictation page**, with the same list.
- **Real model names everywhere**, like "Download Whisper Large v3 Turbo?", and speed and accuracy dots on every model on this computer.
- **Choose the AI that writes for you** on Gusan Cloud: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GPT-5.4 mini, Claude Haiku 5.5, Mistral Small or Gemini 2.5 Flash. The others stay behind it as a backup, so a pick never makes an answer fail. Cloud speech can be Fast or Best accuracy.

### 🔑 Any API key

- **One list of named keys** for Mistral, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, Cerebras or any compatible service. Test a key before you save it.
- **Kept safe.** Each key sits in your computer's own secure storage, one item per key, and belongs to your account.

### 💎 A new Pro window

- **One plan card** with Yearly, Monthly and Lifetime on a single switch. Swipe or drag between the plans. Each card says what it gives and what it commits you to.
- **Free keeps its monthly allowance.** One cloud meeting note and one Live Assistant session each calendar month, and as much as you like with your own key or an AI on this computer.

### 🎧 Audio to text

- **Also save a Word file.** A .docx is saved next to your audio file.
- **A plain privacy line** that says where the audio goes.

### 🍎 On a Mac

- **Gusan, not TranscribeApp.** The Dock, Launchpad and Spotlight show the Gusan name and a sharp Gusan logo.
- **A permissions guide.** It shows Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring with one Turn on button each, updates by itself, and opens when something is off.
- **A paste is never lost.** If Gusan can't paste, the text stays on the clipboard and a notification says how to fix it.
- **New Mac installs use Control+R and Control+S**, because Option plus a letter types a character on a Mac. Windows keeps Alt+R and Alt+S, and existing settings are not changed.

### 🎬 Installing and starting

- **A short video** plays while Windows installs Gusan.
- **A small loading card** shows while Gusan starts on Windows.

### 📦 Downloads named Gusan

- **Windows installer.** `Gusan-Windows-Setup.exe`. The same file is also `TranscribeApp-Windows-Setup.exe`.
- **Windows portable.** `Gusan-Windows.zip`, also published as `TranscribeApp-Windows.zip`.
- **Mac.** `Gusan-Mac.dmg`, also published as `TranscribeApp-Mac.dmg`.
- **What gets installed stays as it was.** The program file is still `TranscribeApp.exe`, in the same folder, so this replaces the Gusan you already have. An older Gusan still finds the TranscribeApp download and updates.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
