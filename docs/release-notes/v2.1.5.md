## Best accuracy in Smart Actions, calmer lists and a shorter history

Gusan 2.1.5 fixes picking Best accuracy for Smart Actions, makes every dropdown open smoothly, shows speed and accuracy for Gusan Cloud too, and keeps your dictation history short. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### 🛠️ Fixes

- **Best accuracy sticks in Smart Actions.** Choosing Best accuracy under "Transcribes with" went back to Fast. Now it stays.
- **Lists open smoothly.** Every dropdown opens in place at once, without shaking or sliding.

### 🎯 Better

- **Speed and accuracy for Gusan Cloud.** Fast and Best accuracy show their dots like the models on this computer: Fast is the quickest, Best accuracy the most accurate.
- **A shorter dictation history.** The Dictation page shows today and yesterday; everything older waits under one "Earlier" row that opens with a click. Search still finds everything.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
