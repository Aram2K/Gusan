## Your avatar, updates in Settings, and what's new after every update

Gusan 2.1.4 lets you choose your avatar, shows its updates right in Settings, and from now on tells you what changed each time it updates. Best accuracy is now truly the most accurate, and meeting notes stay in the language of your meeting. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### ✨ New

- **Choose your avatar.** Hover your avatar in Account & plan and click the pencil: your initial on one of six colours, one of six pictures, or your own photo. It shows everywhere at once.
- **What's new after every update.** When Gusan has updated, a short window shows what changed, each item marked New, Better or Fixed. Open it again any time from Settings › Updates & about.
- **Updates right in Settings.** The download, the check and the install show in Updates & about, with Cancel and Try again, instead of a separate window. You will see it from your next update on.

### 🎯 Better

- **Best accuracy is truly the most accurate.** It now writes with ElevenLabs Scribe v2 first, instead of the live stream Fast uses. It takes a moment longer after you stop.
- **Gusan Cloud comes first** in every model list, and each list shows the model that really runs.
- **Clearer links.** The Dictation page's link now says "Speech & AI settings", where it takes you.

### 🛠️ Fixes

- **Meeting notes in your meeting's language.** A meeting held in English that opened with a few words in another language no longer gets its notes and title in that language.
- **A sharp avatar.** The small avatar is no longer cut at its edges.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
