## Dark mode, Invite friends and better meetings

Gusan 2.1.11 follows your computer's light or dark look, lets you share Gusan with friends for free Pro, and makes meetings safer and clearer: speaker names, headsets that just work, and recordings saved as you go. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### ✨ New

- **Dark mode.** Gusan follows Windows or macOS and switches the moment your computer does. You can also pick Light or Dark in Settings › General › Appearance. Colours are calmer at night, and every text stays easy to read.
- **Invite friends.** Share your link or your code from the new Invite friends page in the sidebar. A friend who joins gets 2 weeks of Pro, and every 3 friends who join give you a month of Pro. If you already pay for Pro, that month comes off your next bill.
- **Got a friend's code?** Enter it on the Invite friends page or in Account, any time. When a friend's link was opened on your network, Gusan offers it to you there.
- **Speaker names in meetings.** After Stop, the transcript, the notes and People show who spoke: by name when Gusan can tell, otherwise as Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on. Rename a speaker once and every line follows.
- **Meetings follow your headset.** Plug in a headset or a microphone, or switch speakers, in the middle of a call: Gusan records from it right away, and the call's sound keeps coming from the device the call plays on.
- **Alt+M starts a meeting** from anywhere (Ctrl+M on a Mac). Change it in the Meetings page settings.
- **Still recording?** After every hour of a meeting, Gusan asks whether to keep recording or stop, so a call that ended long ago doesn't keep running.
- **Promotion codes at checkout.** Monthly and Yearly accept a promotion code.

### 🎯 Better

- **Meetings are saved while you record.** If Gusan is closed or the computer restarts mid-call, the recording is kept and its notes are written the next time Gusan opens.
- **Speed that fits your computer.** The speed dots next to each local model now follow your computer: a graphics card or a strong processor shows the big models as fast as they really run here.
- **Downloaded models show a check**, so every row in the model list lines up.
- **Easier to read.** Small grey text, like times and sub-lines, is a little darker in light mode.
- **A cleaner Account page.** The usage card is gone.

### 🛠️ Fixes

- **Long meeting names** are no longer cut at the start; the end is shortened instead, and the full name shows when you point at it.
- **"Speech & AI settings" shows its full name** on the Dictation page.
- **The Pro window shows Monthly and Lifetime in full**, and the Yearly saving no longer covers its label.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
