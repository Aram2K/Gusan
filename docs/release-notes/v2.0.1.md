## Live meetings that tell who spoke, your own documents, and Notion in one click

Gusan 2.0.1 tells you who spoke in your live meetings, lets the Live Assistant read your own documents and follow your rules, makes the Notion connection simple, and shows clearly which speech model runs where. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### 📝 Notion

- **One button.** Choose **Connect Notion**, approve in your browser, and you're connected. No page to share and no secret to paste.
- **Your own page.** Gusan makes a private "Gusan meetings" page in your Notion and adds each meeting to it.
- **Clear messages.** If your Notion workspace is full, Gusan says so and what to do, instead of only saying it couldn't connect.
- **Already connected with your own Notion secret?** It keeps working, and you can still choose that way from the Notion window.

### 📄 Documents and rules in the Live Assistant

- **Add your own documents.** In the **Context** card on the Live Assistant page choose **Add documents**, or drop files onto the page or onto the card. PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text files, pictures and more.
- **You choose what it reads.** Every document has a **Use in answers** switch, and the card shows how many are on. Remove one and Gusan forgets it.
- **Rules it follows.** Write how you want answers given, and switch on **Apply to everything I hear** to keep a rule on for the whole session. If you delete the rule, the switch turns itself off.
- **Kept on your computer.** Files are only read, never opened or run, and a file Gusan can't read gets a short, plain message. Only the few parts that match a question go to the AI that writes the answer, and Gusan asks you first. With Privacy mode on, nothing leaves your computer.

### 🎧 Live meetings

- **You and the call are told apart.** The transcript says who spoke: you, then Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on for the people on the call.
- **Notes first, names after.** Your notes are ready as soon as you stop. Gusan adds who said what a little later, in the background, while you're not recording or dictating. You can cancel it.
- **A note when Gusan hears nothing for a while.** If your microphone or the call goes quiet for minutes, Gusan tells you, so you can check your sound settings.
- **Identify speakers for saved meetings.** Open a meeting, choose ⋯ then **Identify speakers**, and Gusan adds who said what to its transcript.
- **No more freezing at the end of a long session.** Gusan stays responsive while it finishes a long meeting or Live Assistant session.

### 🧠 Speech models you can read at a glance

- **Local, Cloud or Pro.** Every speech model in every list wears a small label, so you know what runs on your computer and what is sent to Gusan Cloud.
- **The Live Assistant picks its own model.** A new **Transcribes with** choice lets you pick the model, and Gusan keeps to the language you chose instead of switching on its own.
- **Gusan Cloud says what ran.** Choose **Fast** or **Best accuracy**; the line under it, and your history, name the model that actually read your audio.
- **More from NVIDIA graphics cards.** Cards that support it now run Gusan's speech models at full precision for better accuracy, and Gusan steps down on its own if the card runs out of memory.
- **Calmer connection problems.** A cloud speech error now says what happened in plain words, and a short retry keeps a live card going.

### 🗑 Deleting, and pop-ups you can read

- **Delete from the list.** Rows in Meetings, the Live Assistant, Dictation and Audio to text show a small trash icon when you point at them, and an open meeting can be deleted from inside it too.
- **Undo for meetings.** A deleted meeting waits in the trash for 10 seconds, so you can take it back.
- **Readable everywhere.** Menus, tooltips and confirmation windows now use Gusan's own colours, so they stay readable on Windows dark mode.

### 👤 Account

- **A guest sees a guest card.** Before you sign in, the card says **User** and offers **Sign in / Sign up** in blue, not an upgrade button.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
