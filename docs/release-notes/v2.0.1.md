## Send your meeting notes to Notion in one click

Gusan 2.0.1 makes the Notion connection simple. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### 📝 Notion

- **One button.** Choose **Connect Notion**, approve in your browser, and you're connected. No page to share and no secret to paste.
- **Your own page.** Gusan makes a private "Gusan meetings" page in your Notion and adds each meeting to it.
- **Clear messages.** If your Notion workspace is full, Gusan says so and what to do, instead of only saying it couldn't connect.
- **Already connected with your own Notion secret?** It keeps working, and you can still choose that way from the Notion window.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
