## Live captions you can read

Gusan 2.1.7 makes live captions easier to read: words come at a pace you can follow, more of them stay on screen, you pick their size, and lines no longer sit on top of each other. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### 🎯 Better

- **Captions at reading pace.** Every word comes one after another at a pace you can read. The start of a phrase and translated sentences no longer arrive all at once.
- **More on screen.** Three lines for each side of the call, wider lines across the screen, and captions stay a few seconds longer after the last word.
- **Easier to follow.** A new sentence starts on a new line when the line before is already well filled.

### ✨ New

- **Caption size.** Pick Small, Medium, Large or Extra large on the Meetings page, or right on the meeting while it runs.

### 🛠️ Fixes

- **No more lines on top of each other.** A new line waits until the line above has moved up.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
