## Live captions, press or hold, and a paste you can always find

Gusan 2.1.6 puts live captions of your meetings on the screen, lets you pick how meetings are transcribed, adds hold-to-talk dictation, and keeps every result on your clipboard. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### ✨ New

- **Live captions in meetings.** Turn on Live captions on the Meetings page and the words of your call appear at the bottom of your screen, as spoken or translated into your language. Both sides get their own colour, filler words are left out, and you choose the font and colours. Captions are hidden from screen sharing unless you turn that on. Part of Gusan Pro.
- **Transcribes with, for meetings.** Pick how your meetings are transcribed, for each meeting language: Gusan Cloud Fast for real time, Best accuracy, or a model on this computer. Captions follow your choice.
- **Press or hold.** On Windows, choose on the Dictation page: press the shortcut to start and again to stop, or hold it while you talk and let go to stop.

### 🎯 Better

- **Smart Actions show one step at a time.** The bar shows what it is doing right now (transcribing, searching the web, summarizing, making a list), in order, one icon at a time.
- **Your last words are kept.** Dictation listens half a second longer after you stop, so the end of a sentence is not cut.
- **The Google Calendar icon** marks calendar meetings on Home and on the Meetings page.
- **A smoother Earlier row.** The older dictations open and close with a gentle slide.

### 🛠️ Fixes

- **Every result stays on your clipboard.** If a paste does not land where your cursor is, press Ctrl+V and it is there.
- **No more flashing window in meetings.** New lines of a live transcript no longer flash a small window for a moment.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
