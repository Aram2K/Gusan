## Captions that keep up with the call

Gusan 2.1.8 makes live captions follow the voice instead of trailing it, lets you read a meeting in your caption language, uses one engine for everything a meeting shows, and lets meeting notes follow the AI writer you picked. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### 🎯 Better

- **Captions as the words are said.** A word shows the moment it is known, and a finished sentence appears whole. Nothing is held back.
- **Translated captions, a phrase at a time.** Translation starts while the person is still speaking and arrives a few words at a time, in the order spoken.
- **Lines that leave on time.** Each line stays about 10 seconds after its last word and then leaves. Nothing scrolls back or flickers.
- **One engine for a meeting.** The live transcript, the captions and the Live Assistant all use the engine picked in Transcribes with. Fast is real time; Best accuracy shows its own words a few seconds later.
- **Best accuracy for meetings on Pro.** With Transcribes with on Automatic, a Pro account's meetings now use Best accuracy on Gusan Cloud. Pick Fast or a model on this computer to change it; Privacy mode keeps meetings on this computer.
- **Back to your meetings after Stop.** Stop takes you to the Meetings list at once; the meeting sits there while its notes are written and opens on its own page.
- **Notes by the AI writer you picked.** Meeting notes on Gusan Cloud are written by the writer chosen in Settings, and the Meetings page names that model.

### ✨ New

- **The conversation in your caption language.** When captions are in another language than the one spoken, the live transcript gets a switch - Original or that language - and so does the saved meeting's Transcript.
- **A card for live captions.** Caption language, caption source, size, style and "Show while screen sharing" sit together in their own card on the Meetings page.
- **Caption size as a slider.** From 80% to 180%, also on the meeting while it runs.
- **Newer Claude models with your own key.** Claude Sonnet 5.5 (the default), Opus 5.5 and Haiku 5.5.

### 🛠️ Fixes

- **Claude with your own key works again.** Requests to the current Claude models were refused.
- **Smart Actions names the right writer.** The card showed DeepSeek when another writer was picked.
- **No more "Pasted" with nothing pasted.** When the cursor is somewhere nothing can be typed and Gusan can tell, a Smart Action's result is copied and shown in a small card you can copy from. The result is always on the clipboard.
- **Writing a meeting's cloud notes again on the Free plan.** A second try for the same meeting no longer counts as another meeting.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
