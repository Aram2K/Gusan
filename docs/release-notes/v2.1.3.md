## Shortcuts that stay out of your way, cleaner lists, and the web everywhere

Gusan 2.1.3 polishes what 2.1.2 started: your shortcuts never type a stray letter, every dropdown is one clean card, the Live Assistant and Smart Actions look things up on the web without fuss, and Gusan tells you right away when your microphone stops sending sound. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### ⌨️ Shortcuts

- **No stray letters.** Alt+S, Alt+R and the Live Assistant shortcut no longer type their letter into the app in front, for example an "s" in a text box. Windows now takes the whole combination.

### 🎙️ Your microphone

- **A clear message when it sends nothing.** If your microphone stops sending sound (it can happen after the computer sleeps), the take ends after a couple of seconds and Gusan says so, instead of waiting in silence.

### 🔎 The web

- **Smart Actions search when you ask.** "Search Gusan", "look it up", "what AI events are in Paris next week?": Gusan searches and answers with its sources.
- **The Live Assistant too**, with "Search the web for answers" on its page.
- **No more "I can't browse the web".** When search is off, the answer says it can be turned on in Gusan.

### 🎯 Smart Actions

- **Best accuracy for Smart Actions.** "Transcribes with" offers Gusan Cloud Fast and Best accuracy, and each runs the one you pick.
- **"What you can say" is compact and complete:** Email, To-do list, Translate, Polish, Your selection and Search the web, each with a Try it box.

### 🎨 Design

- **Every dropdown is one clean card.** On Windows 11 its corners are rounded and its shadow follows them, like Windows' own menus. Rows are even, and the chosen one is a soft blue.
- **A record button you want to press.** The red button on the Meetings page sits in its own well and sinks in when you press it.
- **The Gusan icon while updating.** The taskbar shows Gusan during an update, not the installer's default icon.

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
