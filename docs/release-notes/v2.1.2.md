## Real time everywhere, your selection, the web, and meetings with real names

Gusan 2.1.2 writes as you speak: a dictation is ready the moment you stop, and meetings show every sentence, with who said it, a fraction of a second after it is said. Smart Actions now work on the text you select and can search the web. Meetings get proper names. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### ⚡ Real time

- **Dictation is ready when you stop.** With Gusan Cloud your words stream while you speak, so the text is there about a fifth of a second after Stop, instead of a few seconds.
- **Meetings and Live Assistant in real time.** Each sentence appears in the meeting and in Live Assistant about a third of a second after it is said, so an answer reacts to what was just said.
- **Who is speaking, live.** Your voice is "You" and every other voice gets its own speaker number while the meeting runs, also when two people share one microphone. The notes still name the speakers precisely after the meeting.
- **Nothing to switch on.** If the live connection drops, Gusan quietly carries on the way it did before and nothing is lost.

### 🌍 Languages

- **Detect automatically keeps each sentence in its own language.** Switch languages in one take and every sentence stays as you said it.
- **A language you set is used as you set it**, and each speech engine is told it. If you clearly speak another language, the text comes out as you said it, never translated.
- **Real engine names** in the Speech-to-text lists, so you see what writes your words.

### ✍️ Smart Actions on your selection

- **Select text, press the Smart Actions shortcut, say what to do.** "Translate it", "make it shorter", "make it more polite": the result replaces your selection.
- **Questions get an answer card** with a Copy button. Where nothing can be typed, like a web page, the result is copied and shown.

### 🔎 Search the web

- **Smart Actions can search the web** with Gusan Cloud. Turn on "Search the web" on the Smart Actions page.
- **Ask, and it searches.** "Search the web for...", "look it up", or a question about events, places or anything current. Say "deep search" for a thorough one.
- **A spinning globe** shows while it searches, and the sources are listed with the answer.
- **Live Assistant and the meeting chat** can search too: "Search the web for answers" on the Live Assistant page. Off until you turn it on.

### 🗂️ Meetings with real names

- **Every meeting gets a short title**, like "Interview with BearingPoint", instead of the date. Older meetings are named too.
- **The list is grouped by day**, with the time and a line of the notes for each meeting.
- **Rename it any time.** Your own name is never replaced.

### 🎧 Audio to text

- **Gladia Solaria** is a new choice for long recordings, and it gets your Vocabulary words.

### 🛠️ Fixes

- **Updating at Quit no longer freezes the window** while the installer starts.
- **Dropdowns open as one clean list** right under their box.
- **Home opens without a pause** when a recent item is very long.
- **The meeting chat never drops part of an answer.**

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
