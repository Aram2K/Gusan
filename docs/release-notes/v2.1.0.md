## New Gusan Pro plans, Coming up, and an update you can see

Gusan 2.1.0 changes how Gusan Pro is sold, puts your next Google Calendar meetings on Home, and shows an update from the first click until Gusan opens again. Your settings, history, meetings and sign-in are kept.

### 💳 Gusan Pro

- **Monthly.** €15.99 a month. Cancel anytime.
- **Yearly.** €9.99 a month, billed every month, for 12 months (€119.88 over that year). You can cancel at the end of those 12 months, not before. After that, billing continues at €9.99 a month until you cancel.
- **Lifetime.** €199 once, for the first 200 people.
- **No trial.** Gusan Pro starts when you pay.

### 🎁 Free

- **One cloud meeting note and one Live Assistant session each month.** Writing the same meeting again that month does not use another note.
- **Smart Actions on this computer are included, as often as you like.** Dictation and audio to text on this computer stay included too. Gusan Cloud speech is part of Gusan Pro.

### 📅 Coming up

- **On Home, above Recent.** Your next Google Calendar meetings sit there: the time (or Now, in 25 min, Today, Tomorrow), the title, the call, and who is coming.
- **Join and Record.** Join opens the call in your browser. Record opens Meetings with that meeting filled in, and does not start recording on its own. See all opens Meetings.
- **Nothing ahead.** It says "No meetings coming up."

### 🔄 Updates

- **A window from the first click.** Updating Gusan shows the download, with Cancel, then Checking the download, then Installing. Gusan closes and opens again by itself when the installer is done. If a recording, a file, or a key you just typed is still open, it waits.
- **Checked before it installs.** Gusan reads the published checksum first. If that check can't be read, nothing is installed, and you can try again. A copy already on this computer is used only when it matches that check.
- **A quiet install, and never two at once.** On the installed Windows copy, Setup shows a small window instead of the full wizard. If an installer is already running, Gusan does not start a second one. On a Mac, a portable copy, or a copy you run yourself, Gusan opens the download page.
- **After it restarts.** Gusan says it is up to date, or that the installer stopped before it finished.

### 📆 Google Calendar

- **A shorter Connect box.** Before the browser opens, one sentence and a few short points: Gusan reads the title, time, guests, agenda and call link, it never changes your calendar, it stays on this computer, and you can disconnect any time in Meeting settings.
- **A privacy link you can open.** Privacy details opens gusan.aibuben.xyz/privacy in your browser. Connect on Home uses this same box.

### 📝 Meetings

- **Notion, right under Google Calendar.** In Meeting settings, Send notes to Notion is the next row.
- **A reminder to record.** The Calendar card says "See your meetings and get a reminder to record."

Download file names still start with TranscribeApp, so updates keep working.
